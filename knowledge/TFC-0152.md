# 9. Testing and Scaling (Part 10)

## TL;DR
Advanced advertising and marketing strategies from Expert Training training.

## Content

Winners campaign Big Idea Migration: the ad's winning message migrates across the whole funnel (landing page, VSL, emails) Frequency management: reduce budget when retargeting frequency goes above 4x Budget reduction as a signal to the algorithm to optimize placement Incremental scaling: 20% budget increases, never doubling at once Two opt-in methodologies: Free Training (faster conversion) vs. Free Report (cheaper leads) Continuous testing: 20-30 new ads per week in groups of 3 80/20 rule: 80% iterate on winners, 20% test new angles Strategic qualification: change copy to focus on "sales teams" (budget indicator) Quick daily analysis of key metrics for optimization decisions STRUCTURE: Column framework: Views → CPM → Frequency → Clicks → Leads → Schedules → Purchases Prioritization hierarchy: CPM affordability → Click cost optimization → Lead quality → Meeting qualification Campaign structure: Cold Traffic (Testing + Winners) → Viewer Remarketing → Lead Nurturing Migration system: Tests (multiple ads) → Winners (few validated ads) Testing pipeline: 3 ads per ad set, multiple simultaneous ad sets, option for Test #1 and Test #2 Budget distribution: $50-$150 daily split between Tests, Winners, and Retargeting Qualification funnel: 44 meetings → 15 disqualified → messaging adjustment toward "sales teams" Big Ideas framework: 1 ad = 1 big idea → if it wins, the idea permeates the whole funnel Support structure: GF School with monthly troubleshooting meetings backed by data Analysis methodology: CPM check → Frequency check → Click cost analysis → Winner identification → Funnel alignment FINANCIAL DATA: Initial investment in the winning ad: $2,000 Current cost per meeting: $153 (profitable) Total spend in the recent period: ~$9,000 New AI angle: $100 invested, 12 leads = ~$8.33 per lead Total daily budget: $150 distributed Recommended minimum budget: $75-$100/day ROI from click reduction: $3 → $1 = 200% more traffic on the same budget Cost per application on winners: $3 and $7 Client 1's problematic CPM: $13 for 31 impressions Established ideal CPM: $40-$60 (achieved on the second client) Acceptable CPM ceiling: up to $80 Disqualification rate: 34% (15 of 44 meetings) Budget increments: maximum 20% per adjustment Final distribution: $100 + $60 + $40 + $125 + $135 = $540 total projected monthly DURATION: ~32 minutes FORMAT: Screencast tutorial with live analysis of multiple Facebook Ads Manager accounts
