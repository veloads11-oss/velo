# Show Rate Optimization

## TL;DR (Heuristic)
Target 70%+ via multi-channel + pre-event value. Paid = 70%, Free = 30-40%.

## Quick Guide
> Golden rule: "Same-day SMS = +15% show rate"

### Show Rate Checklist
- [ ] Automatic calendar invite?
- [ ] Email sequence ready?
- [ ] SMS configured (D-1, D-0)?
- [ ] Pre-event value delivered?

---

## Detailed Framework (Analytical)

### Communication Sequence
| Timing | Channel | Content |
|--------|-------|----------|
| Immediate | Email | Confirm + calendar |
| Day 1 | Email | Welcome video |
| Day 2 | Email | Success story |
| Day 3 | Email | Prep guide |
| -7 days | Email | Final details |
| -3 days | Email | Tech instructions |
| -1 day | Email+SMS | Final reminder |
| -1 hour | Email+SMS | "Starting soon" |
| -15 min | All | "Join now" |
| Start | Email | "We're live" |

### Show Rate Factors
| Factor | Impact |
|-------|---------|
| Paid vs Free | +30-40% |
| SMS reminders | +10-15% |
| Pre-event value | +5-10% |
| Community | +5-10% |

---

## Patterns and Insights (Intuitive)

### Analogy
> "Show rate is like a reservation confirmation - the more you remind people, the more show up"

### Patterns
- Email open rate < 30% = problem with the subject line
- If the SMS doesn't have a direct link, you lose 50% of clicks
- Paid challenge = financial commitment = they show up

---

## Pitfalls (Critical)

### Red Flags
- If show rate < 50%, pause and fix it
- Never rely on email alone
- If you don't have SMS, you're losing 15%

### Common Mistakes
```
MISTAKE: Email only, no SMS
FIX: SMS on D-1 and D-0 are mandatory

MISTAKE: Manual calendar invite
FIX: Automate it at registration
```

---

## Variations (Creative)
- WhatsApp group for community
- "Surprise bonus" for early joiners
- Countdown timer in the email

---

## Key Points
- Paid = 70%+, Free = 30-40%
- SMS is critical for final reminders
- Pre-event value = commitment
- Calendar invite is mandatory
