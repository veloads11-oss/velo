# Paid Traffic Strategy - Scaling Ads

## TL;DR (Heuristic)
Ads make sales easier, they don't replace them. Warm leads up before the call. Scale = strong creative + validated offer + tested process.

## Quick Guide
> Golden rule: "Bad offer + perfect ads = failure"

### Ads Checklist
- [ ] Offer validated before scaling?
- [ ] Creative tested with a low budget?
- [ ] Minimum 3:1 ROAS hit?
- [ ] LTV:CAC ratio >3:1?

---

## Detailed Framework (Analytical)

**Hierarchy of Success in Ads:**
```
1. OFFER (most important)
   |
2. CREATIVE
   |
3. COPY
   |
4. TARGETING
   |
5. TECHNICAL (least important)

"Bad offer + perfect ads = failure"
```

**The Role of Ads in the Funnel:**
```
ADS DON'T SELL - ADS WARM UP

Real function:
- Generate attention
- Qualify interest
- Warm up before the call
- Make the closer's job easier

If the closer has to "sell hard,"
the ads didn't do their job
```

**Ad Metrics:**
```
COST:
- CPM: Cost per 1000 impressions
- CPC: Cost per click
- CPL: Cost per lead

QUALITY:
- CTR: Click-through rate (>1.5%)
- Hook rate: 3-second retention (>30%)
- Hold rate: Overall retention

RESULT:
- CAC: Customer acquisition cost
- ROAS: Return on ad spend
- LTV:CAC ratio (>3:1)
```

## Patterns and Insights (Intuitive)

### Analogy
> "Ads are like hot water - they get the lead ready for the closer to cook"

### Patterns
- If the closer has to sell hard, the ads failed
- CTR <1.5% = weak creative
- Hook rate <30% = the first 3 seconds are bad

---

## Pitfalls (Critical)

### Red Flags
- Never scale without validating the offer first
- Never throw money at ads with ROAS <3:1
- Never blame targeting if the offer is weak

### Common Mistakes
```
MISTAKE: Scaling ads without validating the offer
FIX: Validate with a low daily budget first (see note above on CAD benchmarks pending)

MISTAKE: Focusing on targeting before creative
FIX: Hierarchy: Offer > Creative > Copy > Targeting

MISTAKE: Expecting ads to make the sale
FIX: Ads warm up, the closer sells
```

---

## Variations (Creative)
- UGC ads (user-generated content)
- Aggressive retargeting post-webinar
- Lookalike of buyers (not just leads)

---

## Key Points
- Offer > Creative > Copy > Targeting
- Ads warm up, they don't sell
- Test with a low budget before scaling
- Minimum 3:1 ROAS to scale
