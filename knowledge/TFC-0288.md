# Challenge Pricing Strategy

## TL;DR (Heuristic)
8 weeks: Super EB → Early Bird → Extended → Punishment. 60% of sales happen in weeks 3-4.

## Quick Guide
> Golden rule: "First price = 50% of the final price, raise it 25% each phase"

### Pricing Checklist
- [ ] Super EB defined (anchor)?
- [ ] VIP tier (2x standard)?
- [ ] Bonus for each phase?
- [ ] Price-increase dates communicated?

---

## Detailed Framework (Analytical)

### Pricing Timeline
| Week | Type | Standard | VIP | % of Sales |
|--------|------|----------|-----|----------|
| 1-2 | Super EB | $49 | $79 | 15% |
| 3-4 | Early Bird | $79 | $99 | 60% |
| 5-6 | Extended | $99 | $129 | 15% |
| 7-8 | Final | $199 | $399 | 10% |

### Activities by Phase
```
WK 1-2: Warm list first, limited spots
WK 3-4: Ad launch, aggressive scaling
WK 5-6: Scarcity, "last spots left"
WK 7-8: Maximum urgency, countdown
```

---

## Patterns and Insights (Intuitive)

### Analogy
> "Pricing is like a reverse auction - it starts cheap and goes up"

### Patterns
- If Super EB sells out in 48h, keep the final price low
- Week 3-4 = make-or-break for the challenge

---

## Pitfalls (Critical)

### Red Flags
- 0 sales in week 1 = problem with the offer
- Never discount after Early Bird

### Common Mistakes
```
MISTAKE: Raising the price without adding value
FIX: Every price increase = a new bonus

MISTAKE: Early Bird phase too long
FIX: Max 2 weeks per phase
```

---

## Variations (Creative)
- B2B: $297 → $997
- Low-ticket: $27 → $97
- "Founder's Price" before the Super EB

---

## Key Points
- Add value whenever you raise the price
- VIP = always 2x standard
- 60% of sales happen during Early Bird
- 6-8 weeks before revenue comes in
