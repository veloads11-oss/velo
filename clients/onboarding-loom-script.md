# Client Onboarding — Loom Script: "How to Give Us Access to Your Facebook Ads"

Screen-share on business.facebook.com while recording. Aim for 2-3 minutes.

## Intro (10 sec)
"Hey, quick walkthrough on giving Velo access to run your Facebook
and Instagram ads. This doesn't give us your login or password — you're
just adding us as a partner, and you can revoke it anytime."

## Step 1 — Go to Business Settings
"Head to business.facebook.com/settings. If you don't have a Business
Manager yet, [link/note on creating one]."

## Step 2 — Add Partner
"In the left sidebar, click Users → Partners. Click 'Add' → 'Give a
partner access to your assets.'"

## Step 3 — Enter our Business Manager ID
"Paste in this ID: **1670265237374847**. Click Next."

## Step 4 — Assign assets
"Now select what to share:
- **Ad Account** → check the box, assign us 'Manage campaigns' access
- **Page** → check your Facebook Page, assign 'Manage Page'
- **Instagram account** (if not already linked to the Page) → check it and
  assign access
- **Pixel / Dataset** (if you have one) → check it, assign 'Manage'"

## Step 5 — Confirm access
"Click Confirm/Send. That's it — we'll get a notification and take it
from there."

## Step 6 — Confirm payment method is on file
"Last thing — before we can actually run anything, your ad account needs a
valid payment method attached. Go to Ads Manager → Billing (or Business
Settings → Payment Methods for this ad account) and confirm a card is on
file. This bills directly to you — we never touch or see your card details,
and it means you're never waiting on us to front spend."

## Close
"That's the whole process — no passwords needed, you're in full control of
what's shared, and your ad spend bills straight to your own card. Any
questions, just message me."
